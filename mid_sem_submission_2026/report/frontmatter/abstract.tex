\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}

Football video explains player movement and team shape, while a wearable
explains the physical effort behind that movement. These records are often
reviewed separately. Our project joins them on one session clock and links each
wearable to the correct player track.

The system runs its main processing on the project PC. It uses player and ball
detection, ByteTrack association, team and jersey evidence, pitch mapping, an
ESP32-S3 wearable, signal-quality checks, a FastAPI service, and a responsive
React dashboard. Live remote transport uses the required relay at
\texttt{cpg44.nivaspms.com}. Cloudflare and Caddy route the connection to a
small service that keeps only a bounded reconnect cache and no match archive.

The dashboard gives coaches live tracks, stream health, player workload,
physiological trends, team width, team length, compactness, pressure, and
event-linked review. Injury-related output is shown as a workload concern with
reasons and data quality. It is not a medical diagnosis. The current work also
includes repeatable detector training, firmware builds, software tests, and a
field-validation plan.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} football analytics, player tracking, wearable
sensing, data synchronisation, tactical intelligence, workload monitoring.
